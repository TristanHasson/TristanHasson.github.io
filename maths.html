Hyperbolic Polynomials

Definition: Let \( P(x_1, \dots, x_n) \) be a degree \( m \) homogeneous polynomial on \( \mathbb{R}^n \). If for some \( e \in \mathbb{R}^n \) the univariate polynomial \( t \mapsto P(x + te) \) has only real roots for all \( x \in \mathbb{R}^n \), then we say \( P \) is hyperbolic with respect to \( e \).
