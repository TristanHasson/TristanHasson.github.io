Career Goals

After spending the majority of my adult life studying mathematics with a view to entering academia, I have decided to pivot my career path towards computer science and software development. Programming has been a primary hobby of mine since around the age of ten when I first started learning and experimenting with Python. I'm always looking for a new project to sink my teeth into, from plotting hyperbolicity cones for my PhD to making Discord bots that give weather updates, I relish an opportunity to learn something new. Though I am most comfortable with Python, I have dabbled in various languages and am currently focusing on learning C++. Having spent my teenage years messing around in the Blender Game Engine, I am now looking to turn my career towards game development. Currently learning through working on my own projects, I look forward to where it'll lead in the future.
